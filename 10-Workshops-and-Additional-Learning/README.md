# Workshops and Additional Learning

## Introduction

The internship included learning activities beyond the core bioinformatics modules. These activities provided additional exposure to scientific communication, biostatistics, bioprocessing, laboratory safety, emergency procedures, and practical laboratory environments.

This section documents the workshops, training sessions, safety activities, and laboratory exposure associated with the internship.

---

## Purpose

The additional learning activities were useful for developing skills that complement computational and bioinformatics training.

They provided exposure to:

- Scientific writing
- Biostatistics
- Bioprocessing and bioreactors
- Laboratory safety
- Fire safety
- Emergency procedures
- Mind-mapping
- Laboratory environments
